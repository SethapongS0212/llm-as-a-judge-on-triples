Making a trip plan is a key activity for having a satisfying trip for tourists. However, as we survey, there are many users do not need to spend a lot of time to write the plan, and it becomes a pain point for users. Thus, this work introduces a recommender system that mainly employs the genetic algorithm for generating a trip plan. The approach accepts a few roughly input require- ments from users, and then it creates a whole trip schedule and allows users to modify. A web application for trip planner is de- veloped to demonstrate the suitability and feasibility of the proposed recom- mender system.
